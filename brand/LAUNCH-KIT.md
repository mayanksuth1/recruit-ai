## 5. Say / don't say

- **Say:** "public beta", "free while in beta", "human-approved", "drafts".
- **Don't say:** "fully automated hiring", "replaces recruiters", "unbiased",
  "guaranteed", or quote numbers you haven't measured.
- Never post candidate data, resumes or real client names in screenshots — use
  the demo candidates (`scripts/seed_demo_candidates.sql`).
